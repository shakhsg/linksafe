Common scams targeting Singapore

LinkSafe is tuned to detect these local patterns

🏦

Bank impersonation

Fake DBS, OCBC, UOB, POSB pages asking you to "verify your account" or "reactivate" frozen funds.

🏛️

Government impersonation

Fake SingPass, IRAS tax refund, MOM work permit, ICA passport renewal, HDB and CPF pages.

📦

Parcel & delivery scams

Fake SingPost, Ninja Van, Lalamove tracking links demanding "redelivery fees" or address confirmation.

🛒

E-commerce impersonation

Fake Shopee, Lazada, Carousell pages promising refunds or asking you to "claim a voucher".
